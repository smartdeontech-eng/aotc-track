Offices of the Session

The Session is constituted by agents — the Reverend Ministers and the Catechists — and by presbyters, elected by the congregation. Only an agent may chair, so only an agent may constitute a meeting. Set the names here once and the rest of the app follows; when a new minister arrives you change it in this one place.

Who held an office, and when

A set of minutes is signed by whoever held the office on the date of that meeting, and a quarterly report by whoever held it at the end of that quarter — so reprinting an old document after a change of minister still carries the right name.

Offices marked by arrangement are not in the constitution — the Session and the congregation adopted them — so they carry no constitutional standing.
