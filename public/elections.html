Polling officers

For voters who come without their phone. A polling officer opens the Polling Desk (in the menu once named), takes the voter’s phone number, checks their date of birth or confirms they know them, and sends the ballot to a booth. No code is read out, the officer never sees the ballot, and the voter’s phone gets an SMS once the vote is cast.
