Seed contributions

The lead gift pledged at the launch to encourage the congregation. These bodies become the seed rows on every fundraising day this year — the money is booked against the day it was made available for, whoever paid early.

Harvest week activities

What the week's own activities bring in — a health walk, a bazaar, a games night. Each becomes its own row on every fundraising day this year, counted and reconciled like any other basket, and reported on its own line. Leave it empty if the week has no activities.
